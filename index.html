Check yourself

Is this your moment?

If you're reading this, there's a good chance one of these is you.

You're hiring in Germany

  • You lead HR, recruitment or a training program
  • You need qualified people, not just applications
  • You manage integration and onboarding
  • You work in recognition or qualification bodies
  • You represent health insurance or social protection

You train the next generation

  • You run a school, institute or training center
  • You represent a university or higher education body
  • You teach language or preparation programs
  • You lead a professional association or federation
  • You work in employment or career guidance

You're ready to make the move

  • You're qualified or recently graduated and want more
  • You're considering an Ausbildung in Germany
  • You're already in a recognition process and need clarity
  • You want a real interview, not another maybe

Recognized yourself in one of these? Don't just watch from the sidelines.
